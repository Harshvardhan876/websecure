Privacy Policy

1. Account Data

When you register, we store your name, email address, and a hashed password. We never store your password in plain text.

2. Scan Data

When you run a scan, we store the target URL, the resulting score, header hardening rating, and the individual header/cookie findings, tied to your account, so you can view your scan history.

3. Data We Don't Collect

We do not sell your data, run analytics trackers, or share scan results with third parties.

4. Data Removal

Contact the project maintainer to request deletion of your account and associated scan history.
