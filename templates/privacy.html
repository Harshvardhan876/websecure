Privacy Policy

{% endif %}

1. Account Data

When you register, we store your name, email address, and a hashed password. We never store your password in plain text.

2. Scan Data

When you run a scan, we store the target URL, the resulting score, header hardening rating, and the individual header/cookie findings. If you are logged in, the scan is tied to your account so you can view your scan history.

You can scan without an account. That scan is stored without any personal details, and only your browser can open it, through a signed session cookie. If you then create an account or log in from the same browser, the scan is added to your account.

To limit abuse, we count scans per IP address for one hour. This count is kept in memory only and is never written to the database.

3. Data We Don't Collect

We do not sell your data, run analytics trackers, or share scan results with third parties.

4. Data Removal

Contact the project maintainer to request deletion of your account and associated scan history.
