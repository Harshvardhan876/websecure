What WebSecure checks

Eight checks, weighted to a score out of 100. Each result shows the value the server actually sent.

  1. 1

    Content-Security-Policy

    Limits where scripts can load from, which reduces cross-site scripting. Worth 20 points.

  2. 2

    Strict-Transport-Security

    Tells browsers to use HTTPS only, which blocks downgrade attacks. Worth 15 points.

  3. 3

    Framing protection

    X-Frame-Options or frame-ancestors stop other sites from embedding the page. Worth 15 points.

  4. 4

    Cookie security

    Secure and HttpOnly flags keep cookies off plain HTTP and away from scripts. Worth 15 points.

  5. 5

    X-Content-Type-Options

    Stops browsers from guessing a file's type. Worth 10 points.

  6. 6

    Referrer-Policy

    Controls how much of a page address is shared with other sites. Worth 10 points.

  7. 7

    Permissions-Policy

    Turns off browser features such as camera and microphone that a page does not need. Worth 10 points.

  8. 8

    Information disclosure

    Flags headers that reveal exact software versions. Worth 5 points.

How it works

  1. Create a free account. It is only used to keep your scan history private to you.
  2. Enter a public web address. Private and internal addresses are refused.
  3. Read the report. You get a score, a rating from Strong to Minimal, the evidence for each check, a fix for each problem and a PDF you can download.

What it is

A configuration check. It looks at one response from one page and reports how its security headers are set.

A missing header is a hardening finding, not proof that a site can be attacked.

What it is not

It is not a vulnerability scanner or a penetration test, and it does not try to break into anything. The score rates headers only, and many well-run sites score low because they protect themselves in other ways.

About this project

WebSecure is a student project built to show how security headers work. It stores only your name, email, a hashed password and your scan results. Read the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for the details.
