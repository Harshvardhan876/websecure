1. What WebSecure Does

WebSecure is a passive security configuration assessment tool. It opens the URL you provide once, in a real (headless) browser, the same way a normal visitor would, and checks the security headers and cookie settings of that page against a fixed, project-defined checklist. It does not attempt to exploit, brute-force, scan ports or files, or otherwise actively probe any target. Private and internal network addresses are refused.

2. Your Responsibility

You are responsible for only scanning URLs you own or are authorized to test. WebSecure does not verify authorization on your behalf. Visitors without an account can run a limited number of scans per hour.

3. No Warranty

Findings are configuration observations, not proof of an exploitable vulnerability and not an industry-standard vulnerability score. WebSecure is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind.
